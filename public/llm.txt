# DÁMS Footwear

> Own Every Step. Purely Natural, Locally Made — 100% Original Local Product.

DÁMS is an Abuja-based, family-owned Nigerian footwear manufacturing brand crafting purely natural, 100% original locally produced leather shoes, natural cork slides, derbies, penny loafers, Chelsea boots, and woven mules.

## Brand Identity & Positioning
- **Brand Name**: DÁMS Footwear
- **Headquarters & Workshop**: Abuja (FCT), Nigeria (Wuse II / Maitama Fitting Lounge & Manufacturing Atelier)
- **Tagline**: "Own Every Step. Purely Natural, Locally Made." / "Made Locally. Worn Everywhere."
- **Core Promise**: 100% original local Nigerian product crafted from vegetable-tanned full-grain hides, breathable kidskin linings, and anatomical natural cork and stacked leather soles.
- **Currency**: Nigerian Naira (NGN / ₦)
- **Dispatch & Delivery**: 24-hour delivery within Abuja (FCT); 2–4 business days nationwide across all 36 Nigerian states. Complimentary nationwide shipping on orders of ₦150,000 and above.
- **Fit Guarantee**: Complimentary 14-day size exchange on unworn pairs within Nigeria.
